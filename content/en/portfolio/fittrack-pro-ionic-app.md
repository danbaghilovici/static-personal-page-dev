## Overview

FitTrack Pro is a feature-rich fitness tracking application built with Ionic Framework and Angular. The app provides workout tracking, progress analytics, and social features, all from a single codebase deployed to iOS and Android.

## Key Features

### Workout Tracking
- 200+ pre-built exercises with video demonstrations
- Custom workout builder with sets, reps, and rest timers
- GPS tracking for running, cycling, and outdoor activities
- Apple Watch and Wear OS companion apps

### Progress Analytics
- Visual progress charts for weight, strength, and cardio metrics
- Weekly and monthly performance summaries
- Body measurement tracking with photo comparisons
- Personal records and achievement badges

### Social Features
- Challenge friends to weekly fitness competitions
- Share workouts and achievements to the community feed
- Join group challenges with global leaderboards
- In-app messaging and workout buddy matching

### Offline Support
- Full offline functionality with local SQLite storage
- Background sync when connection is restored
- Offline workout logging and timer features

## Technical Stack

- **Framework**: Ionic 7 with Angular 17
- **State Management**: NgRx for predictable state handling
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express, PostgreSQL database
- **Authentication**: Firebase Auth with social login support
- **Push Notifications**: Firebase Cloud Messaging
- **Analytics**: Mixpanel for user behavior tracking
